You may be ready to modernize when:

  • Aging servers or applications are becoming harder to maintain
  • Manual deployments and data transfers consume engineering time
  • Failures are difficult to detect, diagnose, or recover from
  • Cloud spending has grown without clear ownership or controls
  • Your team needs cloud expertise for a defined transition

A practical migration creates:

  • A workload inventory and measurable business case
  • A staged plan with dependencies and rollback points
  • Automated, repeatable infrastructure and deployments
  • Logging, monitoring, backups, and recovery procedures
  • Documentation your team can operate after handoff
